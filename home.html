What you get

Built for the way FPN actually works.

UPAMS models the real workflow — supervisor allocation, topic gating, chapter corrections, defence scoring — every detail your department already runs on paper, now digital and verifiable.

Smart Allocation

Auto-distribute students to supervisors using round-robin load-balancing, or assign manually. Reassignment supported with full audit trail.

Topic Workflow

Students submit topics; supervisors approve or reject with notes. Supervisors can also assign topics directly — both paths supported.

Chapter Tracking

Versioned uploads for chapters 1–5. Supervisors approve, request corrections, or reject — students see notes immediately and resubmit.

Defence & Scoring

Schedule defences, capture panel scores out of 100 (presentation + project + chapters), and auto-compute grade letter and final mark.

Printable Reports

Generate session-wide reports — student progress, supervisor performance, grade distribution. Export to CSV or print directly.

Audit Trail

Every approval, rejection, allocation, and submission is logged with timestamp, user, and IP. Full accountability built-in.

The journey

From allocation to graduation — in five stages.

Each stage gates the next, so no student moves forward until their supervisor signs off.

1

Allocation

Admin assigns a supervisor to each student for the active session.

2

Topic

Student proposes — or supervisor assigns — and the topic is approved.

3

Chapters

Drafts uploaded one by one. Supervisor reviews. Student corrects.

4

Defence

Supervisor schedules; panel scores presentation, project, and chapters.

5

Result

Final score and letter grade are recorded and reported.
